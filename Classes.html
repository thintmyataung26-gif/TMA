Our Classes

Professional coaching for beginners and experienced martial artists

Home / Classes

DoBu Martial Arts offers a variety of training programmes. Our qualified instructors provide professional coaching in traditional martial arts, self-defence and fitness training.

Karate

Karate helps students improve discipline, confidence, balance and self-defence skills through punches, kicks and traditional kata techniques. Suitable for all ages and belt levels.

Judo

Judo focuses on throwing techniques, grappling, balance and physical fitness while teaching respect and teamwork. An Olympic martial art suitable for all ages.

Jiu-Jitsu

Jiu-Jitsu teaches ground fighting, submission techniques and self-defence strategies for real-life situations using leverage-based techniques.

Muay Thai

Muay Thai develops strength, endurance and confidence using punches, kicks, elbows and knee strikes — the Art of Eight Limbs provides an intense full-body workout.

Self Defence

Our six-week beginners' course (2 sessions per week, £180) teaches practical techniques to improve personal safety and confidence. No prior experience required.

Fitness Training

Improve strength, flexibility and overall fitness with professional programmes. Includes gym access (£6/visit) and personal training (£35/hour).
