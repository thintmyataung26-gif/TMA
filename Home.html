Who We Are

Yangon's Premier Martial Arts Academy

DoBu Martial Arts is a professional training centre providing high-quality coaching for students of all ages and skill levels — from complete beginners to competitive athletes.

We offer Karate, Judo, Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, fitness training and self-defence courses. Our experienced instructors help students improve physical fitness, confidence and self-discipline in a safe, welcoming environment.
